Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery and payment. Can't find yours? Send us a message and we'll help.

Add your items to the cart and check out, or order directly on WhatsApp. We'll confirm your order, delivery address and time before we start baking.

Most orders are baked fresh and delivered within 24 hours. Large or custom orders, like party trays, need at least 48 hours' notice.

We deliver across Lagos. Delivery to other cities may be possible on request, so send us a message. Fees and timing are on our Delivery Information page.

Payment is made before we start baking, by bank transfer or as agreed with us on WhatsApp.

Yes. Tell us what you have in mind, the date and how many guests, and we'll put together a quote for you.

Changes and cancellations are possible before baking begins, so contact us as soon as you can. See our terms for details.

They may contain or come into contact with milk, eggs, wheat, nuts and other allergens. If you have an allergy, tell us before you order so we can advise you.

They taste best on the day they're baked. Keep them in an airtight container in a cool, dry place and enjoy them within a day or two.
