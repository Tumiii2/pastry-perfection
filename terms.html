Orders

An order is confirmed once we've replied to you with the details and the delivery time. We may decline or reschedule an order if we're fully booked.

Pricing & payment

All prices are in Nigerian Naira (₦). Payment is made before we start baking, by bank transfer or as agreed with us on WhatsApp.

Delivery

Delivery fees and timing are explained on our Delivery Information page. We aren't responsible for delays caused by traffic, weather or an incorrect address.

Cancellations & refunds

Every order is baked fresh, so cancellations are only possible before baking begins. If something is wrong with your order, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order details and a photo, and we'll refund you or replace it.

Allergens

Our pastries may contain or come into contact with milk, eggs, wheat, nuts and other allergens. If you have an allergy, tell us before you order so we can advise you.

Privacy Policy

We collect your name, phone number and delivery address only to prepare and deliver your order and to reply to your messages. We don't sell your information, and we only share it with people who need it to complete your delivery.

You can ask us to update or delete your details at any time by contacting us.
